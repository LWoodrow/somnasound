# Somnasound decision register

Statuses:

- `ACCEPTED` — approved constraint; implementation may depend on it.
- `PROPOSED` — preferred direction awaiting evidence or owner approval.
- `OPEN` — alternatives remain under evaluation.
- `REJECTED` — unsuitable under the stated assumptions.
- `REVISIT` — earlier choice reopened because requirements or evidence changed.
